# Trademark and logo use

The name Animal Spirits, the lockup, the bar, the wordmark and the red block with the Keynes portrait are trademarks of Animal Spirits. The files in `logo/`, `cabinet/`, `woodcuts/`, `patterns/`, `devices/`, `social/` and `motion/` are published so that people can refer to Animal Spirits accurately. They are not licensed for other uses.

## You may

- Use the unmodified logo files to link to Animal Spirits or one of its games, in articles, talks, store listings and similar.
- Write "an animal spirits game" next to a game's own name, and link to it.
- Use the favicon and the line "an animal spirits game" in an Animal Spirits game's README, site footer, credits or docs.

## You may not

- Change the drawings: no redrawing, stretching, rotating, flipping, outlining, adding effects, or re-spacing the letters.
- Recolour outside the supplied versions (on dark, on light, single colour).
- Use the name or marks for another studio, game, mod, fork or service, or in a way that suggests Animal Spirits made, sponsors or endorses something it did not.
- Combine the marks with other logos into a new mark, or use them in a domain name or social handle.

## Forks and mods

A fork of an Animal Spirits game must take a new name and drop the marks. Credit the original in words: "forked from animal-spirits/ceteris". Mods may say "a mod for Ceteris" but may not use the block or the lockup.

## The portrait and the woodcuts

The Keynes portrait is a woodcut-style drawing, not a photograph. It was generated as a PNG with Google Gemini from original prompts by Animal Spirits, then traced to SVG with Claude; the cuts in `logo/` are traced from it by `src/animal_spirits_brand/portrait.py`. Because the source image is machine-generated, copyright protection for the portrait itself may be limited in some jurisdictions; the trademark claim on the composed marks does not depend on it.

Questions about a use not covered here: open an issue in `animal-spirits/brand`.

The other woodcuts in `woodcuts/` are made the same way, from prompts by Animal Spirits; the cabinet objects in `cabinet/` are drawn entirely by `src/animal_spirits_brand/engrave.py`.
