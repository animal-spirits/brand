A world economy simulator. Every country, firm and household from 1 January 2000, built from published trade and national accounts, run forward one day at a time.

Fork the world at any date, change one thing, and watch both worlds diverge. Other things equal, until they are not.

starts1 January 2000
worldevery country, its firms, households and governments as agents
dataBACI bilateral trade (CEPII), OECD input-output tables, national accounts
runsdeterministic: same seed and sources, same world, bit for bit
forkschange one thing at any date and run both worlds side by side
platformdesktop app
licenceopen source
statusin development
